CPU VERSUS INTEL - HOW TO USE ON THE i7
1. Connect the drive and close other video processing.
2. Double-click COMPARE-CPU-INTEL.cmd in the shortsflow folder.
3. Wait for the comparison to finish (four rounds of three real clips).
4. The chosen encoder is saved automatically for that CPU model.
5. Send CPU-INTEL-COMPARISON.json to the assistant for interpretation.
6. Start RUN-SHORTSFLOW.cmd normally. Its fast renderer reads the saved choice.

Current i5-6200U measured averages: CPU 29.481 seconds; Intel 31.312 seconds.
This is rendering only, with existing AI choices/transcription reused.
Both use 720x1280, 30 FPS, same captions and 4 Mbps target H.264 bitrate.
Same bitrate does not guarantee identical image quality.
Intel must pass both rounds and be at least 10 percent faster to be selected.
Until this laptop is benchmarked, the automatic mode uses CPU.
If Intel fails during a real render, it retries that clip on CPU.
Whisper and Ollama remain on CPU; this feature accelerates encoding only.
